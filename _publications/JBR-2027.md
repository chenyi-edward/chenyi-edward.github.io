How does the emotion in a CEO's voice relate to investors' responses to an earnings call? This study uses speech emotion recognition to measure vocal valence, arousal, and emotional reversals. Panel-data analyses show that more positive CEO vocal emotion is associated with higher cumulative abnormal returns over the next two trading days. The association is strongest at moderate levels of vocal arousal and is strengthened by more frequent emotional reversals. The findings connect nonverbal corporate disclosure with investor cognitive processing and show why both the intensity and dynamics of vocal emotion matter when interpreting earnings calls.

The article is available online and is assigned to the January 2027 issue of *Journal of Business Research* (volume 218, article 116569).
