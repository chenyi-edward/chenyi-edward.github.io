Concepts are developed through coding exercises and work with real datasets. Practical analysis connects programming choices with data quality, visualization, and model interpretation, building confidence through a sequence of manageable tasks.

Research-paper discussions and collaborative projects link technical skills to empirical inquiry. Clear reasoning and reproducible work remain central, including when AI tools assist with coding or analysis.
