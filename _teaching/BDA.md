Lectures, business cases, and hands-on exercises connect analytical concepts with practical choices. Visual workflows make the steps of an analysis visible, encouraging attention to why a method fits a problem and how data preparation shapes its results.

Collaborative analysis and presentations develop the ability to explain findings, question assumptions, and discuss what the evidence can support. The emphasis is on thoughtful application rather than software operation alone.
