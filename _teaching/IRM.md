Concepts are developed through lectures and case discussions that connect information resource management with organizational challenges. Critical inquiry runs through the course: questioning assumptions, explaining reasoning, and considering the implications of technology in context.

Case analysis strengthens structured argument, while Tech Talks bring current technological developments into the classroom for discussion of their business and social significance.
